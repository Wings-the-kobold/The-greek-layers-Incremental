    CRNG (Current Randomized Number Generations) - Your current RNG that was generated from the machine
    TRNG (Totalled Random Number Generation) - Combined CRNG from all rolls, like adding CRNG's together
    BRNG (Banked Random Number Generations) - Stores the best possible RNG obtained ever.
` ------ Terms -----`

    RWNG (Raw Number Generated) - this is the raw, or more so, 1 / (0 to 1). lower the number, the higher the nRNG
